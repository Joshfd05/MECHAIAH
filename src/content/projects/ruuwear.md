problem: RuuWear is a Sri Lankan clothing label that needed a shop of its own on the web. Customers had to be able to look through the range, choose a colour and size, and place an order without friction. The owners needed a private side of the same site, where they could keep the catalogue, follow each order, and hear from customers, without handing the shop to a third-party marketplace.
built: |-
  A complete online store at ruuwear.lk. Shoppers browse by category, search, save pieces, and check out as a guest or with an account. Each product shows its photos, colours, and sizes, and the cart leads to a clear checkout with bank transfer or cash on delivery. After an order, the customer gets an order number and, for a bank transfer, the details they need to pay, including a direct way to send the receipt on WhatsApp.

  The owners run the shop from a private dashboard. They add and update products, photos, and stock, set the bank details and delivery fee, and follow every order from the shop to the courier to the customer's door. A new order can alert them on their phone. Messages from the contact page sit in the same place, ready to answer. Accounts, password resets, and the shop's own email all sit under the RuuWear name.
